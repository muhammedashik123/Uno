How Ace works
All 52 cards are dealt. Whoever has the A♠ starts. Everyone must follow the suit that was led (A is high). If everyone follows, the cards are thrown away and the highest card leads next. If you can’t follow, cut with any card: the trick stops and whoever played the highest card of the suit picks up all of it. Empty your hand to escape. The last player holding cards is the Kazhutha 🐴.
